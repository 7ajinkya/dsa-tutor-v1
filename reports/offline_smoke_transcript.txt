=== STUDY: What is a graph? ΓÇö study ===

A graph is a way to represent things and the relationships between them. The 'things' are called vertices (or nodes) and the relationships are called edges. Graphs are everywhere: friend networks, road maps, the web, prerequisites between courses. Unlike a list, a graph does not impose a single line of order ΓÇö any vertex may connect to any other.

Example: A metro map: stations are vertices, the tracks between them are edges. The map answers 'what connects to what?' ΓÇö not 'what comes first?', which is why it is not a list.

Watch out: Not everything is a graph ΓÇö a sorted shopping list has no relationships, so 'graph' would add nothing.
